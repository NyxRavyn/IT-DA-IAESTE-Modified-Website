A little bit of life at IAESTE

Who we are

We’re a group of students who believe that the best learning often happens beyond the classroom — and sometimes, beyond borders.

Through IAESTE, we connect students with paid international internships, welcome interns to Jaipur, and bring people together through shared experiences, cultural exchange and a whole lot of curiosity.

More than an internship

A chance to grow, meet people from around the world and come home with stories of your own.

Career development

Work on real projects with companies and research labs abroad.

Cultural exchange

Adapt to new cultures and build confidence and people skills.

Professional network

Join a global family of alumni and host organisations.

Language skills

Practise new languages with people who speak them daily.

Find your place with us

Whether you study at JECRC or somewhere else, there’s a way to be part of the IAESTE community.

Instation

For JECRC students. Access to IAESTE programmes, opportunities and events.

Outstation

For students from other institutions. Access to IAESTE opportunities and resources.
